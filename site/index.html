Groups

Your apps, grouped on the desktop.

Frosted widgets that sit on your desktop. Click a small one to pop its group open; bigger ones launch apps in one click.

  • Any size from 1×1 to 3×3, on the icon grid
  • Drag to rearrange, drop shortcuts in
  • Ctrl + Alt + Space finds any app

Workspaces

One click, and every app is in place.

A button on your desktop that opens a set of apps and lays them out side by side, in columns or in corners, on the screen you choose.

  • Seven layouts, numbered so you choose what goes where
  • Just an icon, or an icon and a name
  • Apps that are already open just move into place

Island

The time, your music, your PC.

A small pill at the top of your screen. It shows what's playing with a live visualizer, opens for controls, and steps aside for full-screen games and videos.

  • Scroll from your music to the time to your PC
  • Headphones and charging show up as they connect
  • Solid black or real frosted glass

Desktop clock

A clock that stays in the background.

A large, thin clock in the middle of the screens you pick, in white or dark to suit your wallpaper.

  • One screen, several, or all of them
  • Small, medium or large, with or without the date
  • Stays behind your windows; clicks go straight through

Light on your PC

Only what you ask for.

Your desktop stays yours

It never moves, changes or deletes your icons, shortcuts or wallpaper.

Nothing watching

It doesn't track your mouse or keyboard, and it never starts with Windows unless you turn that on.

Updates in one click

New versions come from GitHub and are checked before they replace the old one.

Easy to remove

Settings › Remove Nook from this PC, then delete Nook.exe.

Give everything its own place.

One small .exe for Windows 11, with no installer. It isn't signed yet, so if Windows warns you, choose More info › Run anyway.
